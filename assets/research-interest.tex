\section*{Research Interests}

My research interest is in Machine Learning for natural language \& visual Understanding and Generation with emphasis on Multimodal \& Multilingual machine intelligence Safety and Alignment. My focus is developing algorithmic solution or approach to address the risk posed by Foundation Models (video, image, vision-language, audio-visual) \& Multilingual Large Language Models and adopting Economics \& Policy Analysis for Societal \& Economic Measurement of AI Impact \& AI Governance.
